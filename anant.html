📍 Mumbai & Maharashtra Visarjan Guidelines

🚆
Transit & Commuting Expect heavy crowds and local route adjustments near major railway hubs, metro stations, and procession routes across Mumbai and neighboring districts.
🌊
Immersion Locations Prefer designated natural beaches (Girgaon, Juhu, Dadar, etc.) or local municipal artificial ponds to support smooth traffic flow and eco-friendly practices.
🛡️
Safety & Crowd Control Keep children close during large processions. Ensure family members carry identification or contact tags in high-density public zones.
🚦
Traffic Advisories Stay updated with local traffic police alerts regarding closed arterial roads, diversions, and one-way rules for processions.
