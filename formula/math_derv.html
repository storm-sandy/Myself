1. Basic Derivative Formulas

Constant

Derivative of a constant

d/dx(c)=0

Derivative of x

d/dx(x)=1

Power rule

d/dx(xⁿ)=n xⁿ⁻¹

Constant multiple

d/dx[c f(x)]=c f′(x)

Sum rule

d/dx[f+g]=f′+g′

Difference rule

d/dx[f−g]=f′−g′

Derivative of x⁻ⁿ

d/dx(x⁻ⁿ)=−n x⁻ⁿ⁻¹

Derivative of √x

d/dx(√x)=1/(2√x)

Derivative of 1/x

d/dx(1/x)=−1/x²

General power

d/dx(xᵃ)=a xᵃ⁻¹

2. Algebraic Derivatives

Polynomial

d/dx(aₙxⁿ+...+a₁x+a₀)=n aₙxⁿ⁻¹+...+a₁

Quadratic

d/dx(ax²+bx+c)=2ax+b

Cubic

d/dx(ax³+bx²+cx+d)=3ax²+2bx+c

Product rule

(uv)′=u′v+uv′

Quotient rule

(u/v)′=(vu′−uv′)/v²

Reciprocal function

d/dx[1/f]=−f′/f²

Square of a function

d/dx[f²]=2ff′

Cube of a function

d/dx[f³]=3f²f′

Absolute value

d/dx|x|=x/|x|, x≠0
Not differentiable at x=0.

3. Exponential & Logarithmic Derivatives

eˣ

d/dx(eˣ)=eˣ

eᵘ

d/dx(eᵘ)=eᵘu′

aˣ

d/dx(aˣ)=aˣ ln a
a>0, a≠1.

aᵘ

d/dx(aᵘ)=aᵘ ln(a)·u′

ln x

d/dx(ln x)=1/x

ln u

d/dx(ln u)=u′/u

logₐx

d/dx(logₐx)=1/(x ln a)

logₐu

d/dx(logₐu)=u′/(u ln a)

Logarithmic differentiation

If y=f(x), then ln y=ln f(x)
Differentiate both sides to simplify products, quotients and variable powers.

xˣ

d/dx(xˣ)=xˣ(ln x+1)
For x>0.

uᵛ

d/dx(uᵛ)=uᵛ[v′ln u+v(u′/u)]
For u>0.

4. Trigonometric Derivatives

Sine

d/dx(sin x)=cos x

Cosine

d/dx(cos x)=−sin x

Tangent

d/dx(tan x)=sec²x

Cotangent

d/dx(cot x)=−cosec²x

Secant

d/dx(sec x)=sec x tan x

Cosecant

d/dx(cosec x)=−cosec x cot x

sin u

d/dx(sin u)=cos u·u′

cos u

d/dx(cos u)=−sin u·u′

tan u

d/dx(tan u)=sec²u·u′

cot u

d/dx(cot u)=−cosec²u·u′

sec u

d/dx(sec u)=sec u tan u·u′

cosec u

d/dx(cosec u)=−cosec u cot u·u′

sin⁻¹x

d/dx(sin⁻¹x)=1/√(1−x²)

cos⁻¹x

d/dx(cos⁻¹x)=−1/√(1−x²)

tan⁻¹x

d/dx(tan⁻¹x)=1/(1+x²)

cot⁻¹x

d/dx(cot⁻¹x)=−1/(1+x²)

sec⁻¹x

d/dx(sec⁻¹x)=1/(|x|√(x²−1))

cosec⁻¹x

d/dx(cosec⁻¹x)=−1/(|x|√(x²−1))

sin⁻¹u

d/dx(sin⁻¹u)=u′/√(1−u²)

cos⁻¹u

d/dx(cos⁻¹u)=−u′/√(1−u²)

tan⁻¹u

d/dx(tan⁻¹u)=u′/(1+u²)

6. Important Differentiation Rules

Most important

Chain rule

d/dx[f(g(x))]=f′(g(x))·g′(x)

Product rule

(uv)′=uv′+vu′

Quotient rule

(u/v)′=(vu′−uv′)/v²

Function of function

dy/dx=(dy/du)(du/dx)

Parametric differentiation

dy/dx=(dy/dt)/(dx/dt)
Provided dx/dt≠0.

Implicit differentiation

Differentiate both sides with respect to x
Treat y as y(x), so d(y)/dx=y′.

Leibniz notation

f′(x)=dy/dx

Derivative of a composite power

d/dx[uⁿ]=n uⁿ⁻¹u′

7. Higher-Order Derivatives

Second derivative

d²y/dx²=d/dx(dy/dx)

Third derivative

d³y/dx³=d/dx(d²y/dx²)

nth derivative notation

dⁿy/dxⁿ=y⁽ⁿ⁾

nth derivative of xᵐ

dⁿ/dxⁿ(xᵐ)=m!/(m−n)! · xᵐ⁻ⁿ
For integer m≥n.

nth derivative of eᵃˣ

dⁿ/dxⁿ(eᵃˣ)=aⁿeᵃˣ

nth derivative of sin(ax+b)

dⁿ/dxⁿ[sin(ax+b)]=aⁿ sin(ax+b+nπ/2)

nth derivative of cos(ax+b)

dⁿ/dxⁿ[cos(ax+b)]=aⁿ cos(ax+b+nπ/2)

Leibniz theorem

(uv)⁽ⁿ⁾=Σᵣ₌₀ⁿ ⁿCᵣ u⁽ⁿ⁻ʳ⁾v⁽ʳ⁾

8. Derivatives Used in Applications

Slope of tangent

m=dy/dx

Equation of tangent

y−y₁=m(x−x₁)
m is the derivative at the point.

Equation of normal

y−y₁=−(1/m)(x−x₁)
For m≠0.

Increasing function

f′(x)>0

Decreasing function

f′(x)<0

Critical point

f′(x)=0 or f′(x) undefined

Second derivative test

f′(c)=0, f″(c)>0 ⇒ local minimum

Second derivative test

f′(c)=0, f″(c)<0 ⇒ local maximum

Point of inflection

Often where f″(x) changes sign

Approximation / differential

dy≈f′(x)dx
