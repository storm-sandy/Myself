1. Basic Algebra

Distributive law

a(b+c)=ab+ac

Taking common factor

ax+ay=a(x+y)

Fraction addition

a/b+c/d=(ad+bc)/bd

Fraction subtraction

a/b−c/d=(ad−bc)/bd

Fraction multiplication

(a/b)(c/d)=ac/bd

Fraction division

(a/b)÷(c/d)=ad/bc

Percentage

%=(part/whole)×100

Direct proportion

y∝x ⇒ y=kx

Inverse proportion

y∝1/x ⇒ xy=k

2. Algebraic Identities

Square of sum

(a+b)²=a²+2ab+b²

Square of difference

(a−b)²=a²−2ab+b²

Difference of squares

a²−b²=(a−b)(a+b)

Cube of sum

(a+b)³=a³+3a²b+3ab²+b³

Cube of difference

(a−b)³=a³−3a²b+3ab²−b³

Sum of cubes

a³+b³=(a+b)(a²−ab+b²)

Difference of cubes

a³−b³=(a−b)(a²+ab+b²)

Important cubic identity

a³+b³+c³−3abc=(a+b+c)(a²+b²+c²−ab−bc−ca)

When a+b+c=0

a³+b³+c³=3abc

Square of three terms

(a+b+c)²=a²+b²+c²+2ab+2bc+2ca

Product of binomials

(x+a)(x+b)=x²+(a+b)x+ab

3. Factorisation

Common factor

ax+ay=a(x+y)

Grouping

ax+ay+bx+by=(a+b)(x+y)

Difference of squares

a²−b²=(a−b)(a+b)

Perfect square

a²+2ab+b²=(a+b)²

Perfect square

a²−2ab+b²=(a−b)²

Sum of cubes

a³+b³=(a+b)(a²−ab+b²)

Difference of cubes

a³−b³=(a−b)(a²+ab+b²)

Quadratic factorisation

x²+(p+q)x+pq=(x+p)(x+q)

4. Linear, Quadratic & Inequalities

Linear equation

ax+b=0 ⇒ x=−b/a

Two-variable linear equations

a₁x+b₁y=c₁
a₂x+b₂y=c₂

Quadratic equation

ax²+bx+c=0, a≠0

Quadratic formula

x=(−b±√(b²−4ac))/(2a)

Discriminant

Δ=b²−4ac
Δ>0: two distinct real roots; Δ=0: equal roots; Δ<0: no real roots.

Sum of roots

α+β=−b/a

Product of roots

αβ=c/a

Equation from roots

x²−(α+β)x+αβ=0

Vertex of y=ax²+bx+c

x=−b/(2a), y=−Δ/(4a)

Absolute value

|x|=x if x≥0; |x|=−x if x<0

Modulus equation

|x|=a ⇒ x=±a, a≥0

AM-GM inequality

(a+b)/2 ≥ √ab, a,b≥0

5. Sequences & Series

AP nth term

aₙ=a+(n−1)d

AP sum

Sₙ=n/2[2a+(n−1)d]

AP sum using last term

Sₙ=n(a+l)/2

Arithmetic mean

AM=(a+b)/2

GP nth term

aₙ=arⁿ⁻¹

GP sum

Sₙ=a(rⁿ−1)/(r−1), r≠1

GP sum alternative

Sₙ=a(1−rⁿ)/(1−r)

Infinite GP

S∞=a/(1−r), |r|<1

Geometric mean

GM=√ab

Harmonic mean

HM=2ab/(a+b)

Means relation

AM≥GM≥HM

Natural numbers

Σn=n(n+1)/2

Squares

Σn²=n(n+1)(2n+1)/6

Cubes

Σn³=[n(n+1)/2]²

6. Indices, Surds & Logarithms

Product of powers

aᵐaⁿ=aᵐ⁺ⁿ

Quotient of powers

aᵐ/aⁿ=aᵐ⁻ⁿ

Power of a power

(aᵐ)ⁿ=aᵐⁿ

Power of product

(ab)ⁿ=aⁿbⁿ

Zero power

a⁰=1, a≠0

Negative power

a⁻ⁿ=1/aⁿ

Fractional power

a^(m/n)=ⁿ√(aᵐ)

Log product

logₐ(xy)=logₐx+logₐy

Log quotient

logₐ(x/y)=logₐx−logₐy

Log power

logₐ(xⁿ)=nlogₐx

Change of base

logₐb=(log_cb)/(log_ca)

Inverse log rules

logₐ(aˣ)=x
a^(logₐx)=x

Surd multiplication

√a·√b=√ab

Conjugate rationalisation

(a+b)(a−b)=a²−b²

7. Complex Numbers

Imaginary unit

i=√−1, i²=−1

Cycle of i

i⁰=1, i¹=i, i²=−1, i³=−i, i⁴=1

Complex number

z=a+ib

Conjugate

z̄=a−ib

Modulus

|z|=√(a²+b²)

Conjugate product

zz̄=|z|²=a²+b²

Polar form

z=r(cosθ+i sinθ)

De Moivre's theorem

[r(cosθ+i sinθ)]ⁿ=rⁿ(cos nθ+i sin nθ)

Factorial

n!=n(n−1)(n−2)...2·1

Permutation

ⁿPᵣ=n!/(n−r)!

Combination

ⁿCᵣ=n!/[r!(n−r)!]

Pascal identity

ⁿCᵣ=ⁿ⁻¹Cᵣ₋₁+ⁿ⁻¹Cᵣ

9. Matrices & Determinants

2×2 determinant

|a b; c d|=ad−bc

2×2 inverse

A⁻¹=1/(ad−bc)[d −b;−c a]
Requires ad−bc≠0.

Matrix addition

(A+B)ᵢⱼ=aᵢⱼ+bᵢⱼ

Matrix multiplication

(AB)ᵢⱼ=Σₖaᵢₖbₖⱼ

Transpose

(Aᵀ)ᵢⱼ=Aⱼᵢ

Determinant product

|AB|=|A||B|

Inverse product

(AB)⁻¹=B⁻¹A⁻¹

Identity matrix

AI=IA=A

Determinant of inverse

|A⁻¹|=1/|A|

Dividend=Divisor×Quotient+Remainder
