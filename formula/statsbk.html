1. Standard 1–5 — Basic Data & Arithmetic Statistics

Count

Number of objects = count all observations

Total

Total = x₁+x₂+⋯+xₙ = Σxᵢ

Average

Average = Total ÷ Number of values

Mean

x̄ = Σxᵢ/n

Difference

Difference = larger value − smaller value

Range

Range = Maximum − Minimum

Ratio

Ratio = a:b = a/b

Percentage

Percentage = (Part/Whole)×100

Percentage increase

Increase % = (Increase/Original)×100

Percentage decrease

Decrease % = (Decrease/Original)×100

Simple frequency

Frequency = number of times a value occurs

Total frequency

N = Σfᵢ

2. Standard 6–10 — School Statistics

Arithmetic mean

x̄ = Σx/n

Mean with frequencies

x̄ = Σfx/Σf

Weighted mean

x̄w = Σwx/Σw

Median — odd n

Median = value at (n+1)/2 position

Median — even n

Median = [x₍n/2₎+x₍n/2+1₎]/2

Mode

Mode = most frequently occurring value

Empirical relation

Mode ≈ 3(Median) − 2(Mean)

Range

R = L−S
L = largest; S = smallest.

Class midpoint

xᵢ = (lower limit + upper limit)/2

Class width

h = upper boundary − lower boundary

Cumulative frequency

CFᵢ = f₁+f₂+⋯+fᵢ

Relative frequency

RF = f/N

Percentage frequency

% = (f/N)×100

Pie-chart angle

Angle = (frequency/total)×360°

Probability

P(E)=favourable outcomes/total equally likely outcomes

3. Standard 11–12 — Higher Secondary Statistics

Population mean

μ = Σxᵢ/N

Sample mean

x̄ = Σxᵢ/n

Population variance

σ²=Σ(x−μ)²/N

Sample variance

s²=Σ(x−x̄)²/(n−1)

Standard deviation

σ=√[Σ(x−μ)²/N]

Coefficient of variation

CV=(σ/μ)×100%

Quartiles — grouped data

Q₁=L+[(N/4−CF)/f]h

Median — grouped data

Median=L+[(N/2−CF)/f]h

Q₃ — grouped data

Q₃=L+[(3N/4−CF)/f]h

Mode — grouped data

Mode=L+[(f₁−f₀)/(2f₁−f₀−f₂)]h

Bowley skewness

Sk = (Q₃+Q₁−2Median)/(Q₃−Q₁)

Pearson skewness

Sk=(Mean−Mode)/SD

Pearson alternative

Sk=3(Mean−Median)/SD

Covariance

Cov(X,Y)=Σ(x−x̄)(y−ȳ)/(n−1)

Correlation

r=Cov(X,Y)/(sₓsᵧ)

Regression line

ŷ=a+bx

Regression slope

b=Cov(X,Y)/Var(X)

Coefficient of determination

R² = r²
For simple linear regression with an intercept.

4. Descriptive Statistics — Complete Core Formulas

Raw-data mean

x̄=Σxᵢ/n

Frequency mean

x̄=Σfᵢxᵢ/Σfᵢ

Assumed-mean method

x̄=A+Σfd/Σf

Step-deviation method

x̄=A+h(Σfu/Σf)

Geometric mean

GM=(x₁x₂⋯xₙ)¹⁄ⁿ

Geometric mean using logs

log GM=(Σlog xᵢ)/n

Harmonic mean

HM=n/Σ(1/xᵢ)

Weighted harmonic mean

HMw=Σw/Σ(w/x)

AM-GM-HM

AM ≥ GM ≥ HM

Central moment

μᵣ=E[(X−μ)ʳ]

Raw moment

μ′ᵣ=E[Xʳ]

Variance from moments

μ₂=μ′₂−(μ′₁)²

Skewness by moments

γ₁=μ₃/μ₂³ᐟ²

Excess kurtosis

γ₂=μ₄/μ₂²−3

Coefficient of quartile deviation

(Q₃−Q₁)/(Q₃+Q₁)

Coefficient of range

(L−S)/(L+S)

5. Probability — Core & Advanced

Complement

P(Aᶜ)=1−P(A)

Addition theorem

P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B)

Conditional probability

P(A|B)=P(A∩B)/P(B)

Multiplication theorem

P(A∩B)=P(A|B)P(B)

Independent events

P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B)

Total probability

P(B)=ΣᵢP(B|Aᵢ)P(Aᵢ)

Bayes theorem

P(Aᵢ|B)=P(B|Aᵢ)P(Aᵢ)/ΣⱼP(B|Aⱼ)P(Aⱼ)

Expectation

E[X]=Σx p(x) or ∫xf(x)dx

Variance

Var(X)=E[X²]−E[X]²

Covariance

Cov(X,Y)=E[XY]−E[X]E[Y]

Correlation

ρ=Cov(X,Y)/(σₓσᵧ)

Markov inequality

P(X≥a)≤E[X]/a, a>0, X≥0

Chebyshev inequality

P(|X−μ|≥kσ)≤1/k²

Law of total expectation

E[X]=E[E(X|Y)]

Law of total variance

Var(X)=E[Var(X|Y)]+Var(E[X|Y])

6. Probability Distributions

Bernoulli

P(X=x)=pˣ(1−p)¹⁻ˣ, x∈{0,1}
μ=p, σ²=p(1−p)

Binomial PMF

P(X=k)=C(n,k)pᵏ(1−p)ⁿ⁻ᵏ

Binomial moments

μ=np
σ²=np(1−p)

Geometric PMF

P(X=k)=(1−p)ᵏ⁻¹p

Geometric mean & variance

μ=1/p
σ²=(1−p)/p²

Negative binomial

P(X=k)=C(k−1,r−1)pʳ(1−p)ᵏ⁻ʳ

Poisson

P(X=k)=e⁻λ λᵏ/k!

Poisson moments

μ=λ
σ²=λ

Hypergeometric

P(X=k)=C(K,k)C(N−K,n−k)/C(N,n)

Uniform continuous

f(x)=1/(b−a), a≤x≤b
μ=(a+b)/2
σ²=(b−a)²/12

Exponential

f(x)=λe⁻λˣ, x≥0
μ=1/λ
σ²=1/λ²

Normal PDF

f(x)=1/(σ√2π)e^{−(x−μ)²/(2σ²)}

Standard normal

Z=(X−μ)/σ

Gamma PDF

f(x)=βᵅ/Γ(α) xᵅ⁻¹e⁻βx
Rate parameterization β.

Gamma mean & variance

μ=α/β
σ²=α/β²

Beta PDF

f(x)=xᵅ⁻¹(1−x)ᵝ⁻¹/B(α,β)

Beta mean & variance

μ=α/(α+β)
σ²=αβ/[(α+β)²(α+β+1)]

Chi-square

χ²=ΣZᵢ², Zᵢ~N(0,1)

t distribution

T=Z/√(V/ν)
Z~N(0,1), V~χ²ν independently.

F distribution

F=(U/ν₁)/(V/ν₂)
U~χ²ν₁ and V~χ²ν₂ independently.

7. Sampling & Sampling Distributions

Sampling mean

E[x̄]=μ

Variance of sample mean

Var(x̄)=σ²/n

Standard error of mean

SE(x̄)=σ/√n

Estimated SE

SE≈s/√n

Finite population correction

FPC=√[(N−n)/(N−1)]

SE without replacement

SE(x̄)=σ/√n × √[(N−n)/(N−1)]

Standard error of proportion

SE(p̂)=√[p(1−p)/n]

Difference of means SE

SE=√(σ₁²/n₁+σ₂²/n₂)

Difference of proportions SE

SE=√[p₁(1−p₁)/n₁+p₂(1−p₂)/n₂]

Central Limit Theorem

√n(x̄−μ)/σ → N(0,1) as n→∞

Approximate sample size for mean

n≈(z*σ/E)²

Sample size for proportion

n≈z²p(1−p)/E²

Maximum proportion sample size

If p unknown, use p=0.5: n≈z²/(4E²)

8. Estimation & Confidence Intervals

Confidence interval — known σ

x̄ ± zα/2 σ/√n

Confidence interval — unknown σ

x̄ ± tα/2,n−1 s/√n

Proportion CI

p̂ ± zα/2√[p̂(1−p̂)/n]

Difference of means CI

(x̄₁−x̄₂) ± critical value × SE

Difference of proportions CI

(p̂₁−p̂₂) ± zα/2 SE

Paired mean difference

d̄ ± tα/2,n−1 s_d/√n

Variance CI

[(n−1)s²/χ²_upper, (n−1)s²/χ²_lower]

Hypothesis test statistic

Test statistic=(estimate−null value)/SE

One-sample z test

z=(x̄−μ₀)/(σ/√n)

One-sample t test

t=(x̄−μ₀)/(s/√n)

Two-sample t statistic

t=(x̄₁−x̄₂−Δ₀)/SE

Paired t statistic

t=(d̄−d₀)/(s_d/√n)

Chi-square variance test

χ²=(n−1)s²/σ₀²

Type I error

α=P(reject H₀|H₀ true)

Type II error

β=P(fail to reject H₀|H₁ true)

Power

Power=1−β

9. ANOVA & Experimental Design

Total sum of squares

SST=ΣᵢΣⱼ(yᵢⱼ−ȳ..)²

Between-group sum of squares

SSB=Σᵢnᵢ(ȳᵢ.−ȳ..)²

Within-group sum of squares

SSW=ΣᵢΣⱼ(yᵢⱼ−ȳᵢ.)²

ANOVA decomposition

SST=SSB+SSW

Mean square between

MSB=SSB/(k−1)

Mean square within

MSW=SSW/(N−k)

F statistic

F=MSB/MSW

Two-way ANOVA

SST=SSA+SSB+SSAB+SSE

Interaction mean square

MSAB=SSAB/df_AB

Randomized block design

SST=SS_treatments+SS_blocks+SS_error

Coefficient of determination

η²=SSB/SST

10. Correlation, Regression & Generalized Models

Pearson correlation

r=Σ(x−x̄)(y−ȳ)/√[Σ(x−x̄)²Σ(y−ȳ)²]

Simple regression

ŷ=β₀+β₁x

OLS coefficient

β̂=(XᵀX)⁻¹Xᵀy

Residual

e=y−ŷ

SSE

SSE=Σeᵢ²

MSE

MSE=SSE/(n−p)

R²

R²=1−SSE/SST

Adjusted R²

R²adj=1−(1−R²)(n−1)/(n−p−1)

Standard error of slope

SE(β̂₁)=s/√Σ(xᵢ−x̄)²
For simple regression, with s²=SSE/(n−2).

t test for coefficient

t=(β̂ⱼ−βⱼ,0)/SE(β̂ⱼ)

Ridge regression

β̂=(XᵀX+λI)⁻¹Xᵀy

Lasso objective

minβ SSE+λΣ|βⱼ|

Logistic regression

log[p/(1−p)]=β₀+Xβ

Odds ratio

OR=e^βⱼ

Poisson regression

log(μ)=β₀+Xβ

Deviance

D=2[log L(saturated)−log L(fitted)]

MAₜ=(1/k)Σᵢ₌₀ᵏ⁻¹xₜ₋ᵢ

13. Bayesian Statistics

Bayes theorem

P(θ|D)=P(D|θ)P(θ)/P(D)

Posterior proportionality

Posterior ∝ Likelihood × Prior

Evidence

P(D)=∫P(D|θ)P(θ)dθ

Log posterior

log P(θ|D)=log P(D|θ)+log P(θ)−log P(D)

MAP estimate

θ̂MAP=argmaxθ P(θ|D)

MLE

θ̂MLE=argmaxθ P(D|θ)

Posterior mean

E[θ|D]=∫θP(θ|D)dθ

Posterior variance

Var(θ|D)=E[θ²|D]−E[θ|D]²

Conjugate Beta-Binomial

Prior Beta(α,β), data x successes/n
Posterior Beta(α+x,β+n−x)

Conjugate Normal-Normal

Posterior precision = prior precision + data precision

f(t)=−dS(t)/dt

Hazard function

h(t)=f(t)/S(t)

Cumulative hazard

H(t)=∫₀ᵗh(u)du

Survival-hazard relation

S(t)=e⁻ᴴ⁽ᵗ⁾

Kaplan-Meier estimator

Ŝ(t)=Π_{tᵢ≤t}(1−dᵢ/nᵢ)

Exponential survival

S(t)=e⁻λt

Weibull hazard

h(t)=(k/λ)(t/λ)ᵏ⁻¹

Cox proportional hazards

h(t|x)=h₀(t)e^{βᵀx}

Hazard ratio

HR=e^{βⱼ}

15. Research / PhD-Level Statistics

Likelihood

L(θ|x)=Πᵢf(xᵢ|θ)

Log-likelihood

ℓ(θ)=Σᵢlog f(xᵢ|θ)

Score function

U(θ)=∂ℓ(θ)/∂θ

Observed information

J(θ)=−∂²ℓ(θ)/∂θ∂θᵀ

Fisher information

I(θ)=E[U(θ)U(θ)ᵀ]=−E[∂²ℓ/∂θ∂θᵀ]

Asymptotic MLE distribution

√n(θ̂−θ₀) → N(0,I(θ₀)⁻¹)

Wald statistic

W=(θ̂−θ₀)ᵀVar(θ̂)⁻¹(θ̂−θ₀)

Likelihood-ratio statistic

LR=2[ℓ(θ̂_full)−ℓ(θ̂_restricted)]

Score test

LM=U(θ̃)ᵀI(θ̃)⁻¹U(θ̃)

Delta method

√n[g(θ̂)−g(θ)] → N(0, ∇gᵀΣ∇g)

Bootstrap standard error

SE_boot≈SD(θ̂*₁,...,θ̂*ᴮ)

Bootstrap percentile interval

CI≈[quantile(θ̂*, α/2), quantile(θ̂*,1−α/2)]

Empirical CDF

Fₙ(x)=(1/n)ΣI(Xᵢ≤x)

Law of large numbers

x̄ₙ → μ (under standard LLN assumptions)

Central limit theorem

√n(x̄−μ)/σ → N(0,1)

Slutsky theorem

If Xₙ→dX and Yₙ→p c, then XₙYₙ→d cX

Continuous mapping

Xₙ→X ⇒ g(Xₙ)→g(X), under continuity

Covariance matrix PSD

aᵀΣa ≥ 0 for every vector a

Multivariate delta method

√n[g(θ̂)−g(θ)]→N(0,JΣJᵀ)

Asymptotic efficiency

Var(θ̂) approaches I(θ)⁻¹/n under regularity conditions

AIC

AIC=−2ℓ̂+2k

BIC

BIC=−2ℓ̂+k ln n

Deviance

D=2(ℓ_saturated−ℓ_fitted)

False discovery rate

FDR=E[V/R]
V=false positives; R=total rejected hypotheses.

Bonferroni correction

α* = α/m

Benjamini-Hochberg threshold

Reject up to largest i with p₍i₎ ≤ (i/m)q

Entropy

H(X)=−Σp(x)log p(x)

KL divergence

D_KL(P||Q)=ΣP(x)log[P(x)/Q(x)]
