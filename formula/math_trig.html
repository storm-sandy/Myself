1. Basic Trigonometry

SOH

Sine

sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse
CAH

Cosine

cos θ = Adjacent / Hypotenuse
TOA

Tangent

tan θ = Opposite / Adjacent

Cosecant

cosec θ = 1/sin θ = H/O

Secant

sec θ = 1/cos θ = H/A

Cotangent

cot θ = 1/tan θ = A/O

Relation of tan

tan θ = sin θ / cos θ

Relation of cot

cot θ = cos θ / sin θ

Reciprocal relations

sinθ·cosecθ=1
cosθ·secθ=1
tanθ·cotθ=1

2. Fundamental Trigonometric Identities

Pythagorean identity

sin²θ + cos²θ = 1

Secant identity

1 + tan²θ = sec²θ

Cosecant identity

1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ

Derived identity

sec²θ − tan²θ = 1

Derived identity

cosec²θ − cot²θ = 1

Derived identity

sin²θ = 1 − cos²θ

Derived identity

cos²θ = 1 − sin²θ

Derived identity

tan²θ = sec²θ − 1

5. Compound Angle Formulas

Sine of sum

sin(A+B)=sinA cosB + cosA sinB

Sine of difference

sin(A−B)=sinA cosB − cosA sinB

Cosine of sum

cos(A+B)=cosA cosB − sinA sinB

Cosine of difference

cos(A−B)=cosA cosB + sinA sinB

Tangent of sum

tan(A+B)=(tanA+tanB)/(1−tanA tanB)

Tangent of difference

tan(A−B)=(tanA−tanB)/(1+tanA tanB)

Cotangent of sum

cot(A+B)=(cotA cotB−1)/(cotA+cotB)

Cotangent of difference

cot(A−B)=(cotA cotB+1)/(cotB−cotA)

6. Double, Triple & Half Angles

Sine double angle

sin2A=2sinA cosA

Cosine double angle

cos2A=cos²A−sin²A

Cosine double angle forms

cos2A=2cos²A−1
cos2A=1−2sin²A

Tangent double angle

tan2A=2tanA/(1−tan²A)

Triple sine

sin3A=3sinA−4sin³A

Triple cosine

cos3A=4cos³A−3cosA

Triple tangent

tan3A=(3t−t³)/(1−3t²), t=tanA

Half-angle sine

sin(A/2)=±√[(1−cosA)/2]

Half-angle cosine

cos(A/2)=±√[(1+cosA)/2]

Half-angle tangent

tan(A/2)=±√[(1−cosA)/(1+cosA)]

Alternative half-angle tangent

tan(A/2)=sinA/(1+cosA)=(1−cosA)/sinA

Cosine rule

Cosine rule — angle

Equilateral triangle

8. Inverse Trigonometry

Inverse sine

y=sin⁻¹x ⇔ siny=x
Principal range: −π/2 ≤ y ≤ π/2.

Inverse cosine

y=cos⁻¹x ⇔ cosy=x
Principal range: 0 ≤ y ≤ π.

Inverse tangent

y=tan⁻¹x ⇔ tany=x
Principal range: −π/2 < y < π/2.

Inverse relation

sin(sin⁻¹x)=x

Inverse relation

cos(cos⁻¹x)=x

Inverse relation

tan(tan⁻¹x)=x

Important identity

sin⁻¹x + cos⁻¹x = π/2

Tangent inverse sum

tan⁻¹x+tan⁻¹y=tan⁻¹[(x+y)/(1−xy)]
Principal-value conditions and branch adjustments apply.

Tangent inverse difference

tan⁻¹x−tan⁻¹y=tan⁻¹[(x−y)/(1+xy)]
Principal-value conditions and branch adjustments apply.

9. Advanced Trigonometry

Product to sum — sin sin

2sinA sinB=cos(A−B)−cos(A+B)

Product to sum — cos cos

2cosA cosB=cos(A−B)+cos(A+B)

Product to sum — sin cos

2sinA cosB=sin(A+B)+sin(A−B)

Product to sum — cos sin

2cosA sinB=sin(A+B)−sin(A−B)

Sum to product — sine

sinA+sinB=2sin[(A+B)/2]cos[(A−B)/2]

Difference to product — sine

sinA−sinB=2cos[(A+B)/2]sin[(A−B)/2]

Sum to product — cosine

cosA+cosB=2cos[(A+B)/2]cos[(A−B)/2]

Difference to product — cosine

cosA−cosB=−2sin[(A+B)/2]sin[(A−B)/2]

General sine equation

sinx=sina ⇒ x=nπ+(−1)ⁿa

General cosine equation

cosx=cosa ⇒ x=2nπ±a

General tangent equation

tanx=tana ⇒ x=nπ+a

Radian arc length

s=rθ
θ must be in radians.

Sector area in radians

A=½r²θ

Euler's formula

e^(iθ)=cosθ+i sinθ

Euler identity

e^(iπ)+1=0

Derivative of secant

d/dx(cosecx)=−cosecx cotx

∫sinx dx=−cosx+C

Integral of cosine

∫cosx dx=sinx+C

Integral of sec²x

∫sec²x dx=tanx+C

Integral of cosec²x

∫cosec²x dx=−cotx+C

∫secx tanx dx=secx+C

Integral of cosecx cotx

∫cosecx cotx dx=−cosecx+C
