Mean • Median • Mode • Range • Variance • SD • Probability • Correlation • Regression • Sampling • Hypothesis Testing

1. Basic Data & Frequency

Number of observations

n = total number of observations

Sum / Total

Σx = x₁+x₂+⋯+xₙ

Frequency total

N = Σf

Relative frequency

RF = f/N

Percentage frequency

Percentage = (f/N) × 100

Cumulative frequency

CF = f₁+f₂+⋯+fᵢ

Class midpoint

xᵢ = (Lower limit + Upper limit)/2

Class width

h = Upper boundary − Lower boundary

2. Measures of Central Tendency

Arithmetic Mean

x̄ = Σx/n

Population Mean

μ = Σx/N

Frequency Mean

x̄ = Σfx/Σf

Weighted Mean

x̄w = Σwx/Σw

Mean — Assumed Mean

x̄ = A + Σfd/Σf

Mean — Step Deviation

x̄ = A + h(Σfu/Σf)

Geometric Mean

GM = (x₁x₂⋯xₙ)¹⁄ⁿ

Geometric Mean using logs

log(GM) = Σlog(x)/n

Harmonic Mean

HM = n / Σ(1/x)

AM-GM-HM

AM ≥ GM ≥ HM

Median — Odd n

Position = (n+1)/2

Median — Even n

Median = [x₍n/2₎ + x₍n/2+1₎]/2

Grouped Median

Median = L + [(N/2 − CF)/f]h

Mode

Most frequently occurring value

Grouped Mode

Mode = L + [(f₁−f₀)/(2f₁−f₀−f₂)]h

Empirical relation

Mode ≈ 3Median − 2Mean

3. Measures of Dispersion

Range

R = Maximum − Minimum

Coefficient of Range

(L−S)/(L+S)

Population Variance

σ² = Σ(x−μ)²/N

Sample Variance

s² = Σ(x−x̄)²/(n−1)

Variance shortcut

σ² = E(X²) − [E(X)]²

Standard Deviation

σ = √[Σ(x−μ)²/N]

Sample Standard Deviation

s = √[Σ(x−x̄)²/(n−1)]

Coefficient of Variation

CV = (SD/Mean) × 100%

Quartile Deviation

QD = (Q₃−Q₁)/2

Coefficient of QD

(Q₃−Q₁)/(Q₃+Q₁)

Interquartile Range

IQR = Q₃ − Q₁

Mean Absolute Deviation

MAD = Σ|x−A|/n
A may be mean or median depending on the definition used.

4. Position Measures

Quartiles

Q₁ = 25th percentile
Q₂ = 50th percentile
Q₃ = 75th percentile

Percentile position

Position ≈ P(n+1)/100

Deciles

Dₖ = (10k)th percentile

Quartile — grouped

Qₖ = L + [(kN/4−CF)/f]h

Decile — grouped

Dₖ = L + [(kN/10−CF)/f]h

Percentile — grouped

Pₖ = L + [(kN/100−CF)/f]h

5. Skewness & Kurtosis

Pearson Skewness

Sk = (Mean − Mode)/SD

Pearson alternative

Sk = 3(Mean − Median)/SD

Bowley Skewness

Sk = (Q₃+Q₁−2Median)/(Q₃−Q₁)

Moment skewness

γ₁ = μ₃ / μ₂³ᐟ²

Moment kurtosis

β₂ = μ₄/μ₂²

Excess kurtosis

γ₂ = μ₄/μ₂² − 3

6. Probability Basics

Probability

P(A) = Favourable outcomes / Total equally likely outcomes

Probability range

0 ≤ P(A) ≤ 1

Complement

P(Aᶜ) = 1 − P(A)

Addition Rule

P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B)

Mutually exclusive events

P(A∩B)=0
P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)

Conditional Probability

P(A|B)=P(A∩B)/P(B)

Multiplication Rule

P(A∩B)=P(A|B)P(B)

Independent Events

P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B)

Bayes' Theorem

P(A|B)=P(B|A)P(A)/P(B)

Expected Value

E(X)=ΣxP(X=x)

Variance of random variable

Var(X)=E(X²)−[E(X)]²

7. Covariance & Correlation

Covariance

Cov(X,Y)=E[(X−μₓ)(Y−μᵧ)]

Shortcut covariance

Cov(X,Y)=E(XY)−E(X)E(Y)

Pearson Correlation

r = Cov(X,Y)/(sₓsᵧ)

Pearson correlation — raw form

r=[nΣxy−ΣxΣy]/√[(nΣx²−(Σx)²)(nΣy²−(Σy)²)]

Correlation range

−1 ≤ r ≤ +1

Coefficient of determination

R² = r²
For simple linear regression with an intercept.

8. Regression

Simple Linear Regression

ŷ = a + bx

Regression slope

b = Cov(X,Y)/Var(X)

Regression intercept

a = ȳ − bx̄

Residual

e = y − ŷ

Residual Sum of Squares

SSE = Σ(y−ŷ)²

Total Sum of Squares

SST = Σ(y−ȳ)²

Explained Sum of Squares

SSR = Σ(ŷ−ȳ)²

Regression decomposition

SST = SSR + SSE

R-squared

R² = SSR/SST = 1−SSE/SST

9. Sampling & Standard Error

Mean of sample means

E(x̄)=μ

Variance of sample mean

Var(x̄)=σ²/n

Standard Error of Mean

SE(x̄)=σ/√n

Estimated SE

SE(x̄)≈s/√n

Proportion SE

SE(p̂)=√[p(1−p)/n]

Difference of means SE

SE=√(σ₁²/n₁ + σ₂²/n₂)

Central Limit Theorem

√n(x̄−μ)/σ → N(0,1)

Sample size for mean

n≈(zσ/E)²

Sample size for proportion

n≈z²p(1−p)/E²

10. Confidence Intervals & Hypothesis Testing

General Confidence Interval

Estimate ± Critical value × SE

Mean CI — known σ

x̄ ± zα/2(σ/√n)

Mean CI — unknown σ

x̄ ± tα/2(s/√n)

Proportion CI

p̂ ± zα/2√[p̂(1−p̂)/n]

Z Test

z=(x̄−μ₀)/(σ/√n)

One-sample t Test

t=(x̄−μ₀)/(s/√n)

Paired t Test

t=(d̄−d₀)/(sᵈ/√n)

Type I Error

α=P(Reject H₀ | H₀ true)

Type II Error

β=P(Fail to reject H₀ | H₁ true)

Power

Power = 1−β

Significance level

α commonly = 0.05 or 0.01

11. Chi-Square & ANOVA Basics

Chi-Square Statistic

χ² = Σ(O−E)²/E

Expected frequency

E = (Row total × Column total)/Grand total

Chi-square degrees of freedom

df=(r−1)(c−1)

ANOVA total variation

SST = SSB + SSW

Between-group mean square

MSB = SSB/(k−1)

Within-group mean square

MSW = SSW/(N−k)

ANOVA F statistic

F = MSB/MSW

12. Common Distribution Formulas

Bernoulli

P(X=x)=pˣ(1−p)¹⁻ˣ
μ=p, σ²=p(1−p)

Binomial

P(X=k)=C(n,k)pᵏ(1−p)ⁿ⁻ᵏ
μ=np, σ²=np(1−p)

Poisson

P(X=k)=e⁻λ λᵏ/k!
μ=λ, σ²=λ

Normal

f(x)=1/(σ√2π)e^{−(x−μ)²/(2σ²)}

Standard Normal

Z=(X−μ)/σ

Uniform

f(x)=1/(b−a)
μ=(a+b)/2
σ²=(b−a)²/12

Exponential

f(x)=λe⁻λx
μ=1/λ, σ²=1/λ²

s = sample SD

Distribution

f(x)=PDF
