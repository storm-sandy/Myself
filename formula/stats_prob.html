1. Basic Probability

Probability of an event

P(E) = Number of favourable outcomes / Total number of equally likely outcomes

Range of probability

0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1

Impossible event

P(∅) = 0

Sure event

P(S) = 1

Complement

P(E') = 1 − P(E)

Complement form

P(E) + P(E') = 1

Odds in favour

Odds = favourable : unfavourable

Odds against

Odds against = unfavourable : favourable

Empirical probability

P(E) ≈ frequency of E / total trials
Used when probability is estimated from experiments.

Expected frequency

Expected frequency = n × P(E)

2. Counting Principles

Fundamental counting principle

If one task has m ways and another has n ways:
Total = m × n

Factorial

n! = n(n−1)(n−2)...3×2×1
0! = 1

Permutation

nPᵣ = n!/(n−r)!

Combination

nCᵣ = n!/[r!(n−r)!]

Relation

nPᵣ = r! × nCᵣ

Symmetry of combinations

nCᵣ = nCₙ₋ᵣ

Pascal relation

nCᵣ = (n−1)Cᵣ + (n−1)Cᵣ₋₁

Combination with repetition

Number = C(n+r−1,r)
For selecting r objects from n types when repetition is allowed.

3. Conditional & Compound Probability

Conditional probability

P(A|B) = P(A∩B) / P(B)
P(B) ≠ 0.

Intersection from conditional probability

P(A∩B) = P(A|B)P(B)

Multiplication theorem

P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B|A)

Alternative multiplication form

P(A∩B)=P(B)P(A|B)

Independent events

P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B)

Independence condition

P(A|B)=P(A)

Union — general rule

P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B)

Mutually exclusive events

P(A∩B)=0

Union of mutually exclusive events

P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)

Three-event inclusion-exclusion

P(A∪B∪C)=P(A)+P(B)+P(C)−P(A∩B)−P(B∩C)−P(C∩A)+P(A∩B∩C)

Complement of a union

P((A∪B)')=P(A'∩B')

Complement of an intersection

P((A∩B)')=P(A'∪B')

4. Bayes Theorem & Total Probability

Bayes

Bayes theorem

P(A|B)=P(B|A)P(A)/P(B)

Bayes with partition

P(Aᵢ|B)=P(B|Aᵢ)P(Aᵢ) / ΣP(B|Aⱼ)P(Aⱼ)

Total probability theorem

P(B)=Σ P(B|Aᵢ)P(Aᵢ)
A₁,A₂,... form a mutually exclusive and exhaustive partition.

Two-case total probability

P(B)=P(B|A)P(A)+P(B|A')P(A')

Posterior odds

Posterior odds = Prior odds × Likelihood ratio

5. Random Variables & Probability Distributions

Discrete probability distribution

ΣP(X=xᵢ)=1

Expected value

E(X)=ΣxᵢP(X=xᵢ)

Expected value of a function

E[g(X)]=Σg(xᵢ)P(X=xᵢ)

Variance

Var(X)=E(X²)−[E(X)]²

Standard deviation

σ=√Var(X)

Linearity of expectation

E(aX+b)=aE(X)+b

Variance scaling

Var(aX+b)=a²Var(X)
Binomial

Binomial probability

P(X=k)=C(n,k)pᵏ(1−p)ⁿ⁻ᵏ

Binomial mean

E(X)=np

Binomial variance

Var(X)=np(1−p)

Binomial standard deviation

σ=√[np(1−p)]
Geometric

Geometric probability

P(X=k)=(1−p)ᵏ⁻¹p

Geometric mean

E(X)=1/p

Geometric variance

Var(X)=(1−p)/p²
Poisson

Poisson probability

P(X=k)=e⁻λ λᵏ/k!

Poisson mean and variance

E(X)=λ
Var(X)=λ

6. Standard School Examples

Coin — one toss

P(H)=1/2
P(T)=1/2

Two coins — exactly one head

P(exactly 1 H)=2/4=1/2

Die — one number

P(any specified number)=1/6

Die — even number

P(even)=3/6=1/2

Die — number greater than 4

P(5 or 6)=2/6=1/3

Deck of cards

52 cards
4 suits × 13 cards
26 red + 26 black

One ace from a deck

P(Ace)=4/52=1/13

One king from a deck

P(King)=4/52=1/13

Red card

P(red)=26/52=1/2

Queen

P(Queen)=4/52=1/13
