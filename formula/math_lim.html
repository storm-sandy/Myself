1. Basic Concepts & Limit Laws

Definition

Limit notation

limₓ→a f(x)=L
Means f(x) approaches L as x approaches a.

Constant

limₓ→a c=c

Identity function

limₓ→a x=a

Sum law

lim(f+g)=lim f+lim g

Difference law

lim(f−g)=lim f−lim g

Constant multiple

lim(cf)=c·lim f

Product law

lim(fg)=(lim f)(lim g)

Quotient law

lim(f/g)=(lim f)/(lim g)
Denominator limit must be nonzero.

Power law

lim[f(x)]ⁿ=[lim f(x)]ⁿ

Root law

lim ⁿ√f(x)=ⁿ√(lim f(x))
Subject to real-domain conditions.

2. Algebraic Limits

Polynomial limit

limₓ→a P(x)=P(a)

Rational function

limₓ→a P(x)/Q(x)=P(a)/Q(a)
If Q(a)≠0.

Factor cancellation

limₓ→a [(x−a)g(x)]/(x−a)=g(a)
For x≠a near a, when g is defined.

Difference of powers

(xⁿ−aⁿ)=(x−a)(xⁿ⁻¹+xⁿ⁻²a+...+aⁿ⁻¹)

Sum of odd powers

xⁿ+aⁿ is divisible by x+a when n is odd

Conjugate rationalization

(√A−√B)(√A+√B)=A−B

Common 0/0 form

0/0 → factorize, rationalize, standard limits or L'Hôpital

Continuity shortcut

If f is continuous at a, limₓ→a f(x)=f(a)

6. Limits at Infinity

Reciprocal power

limₓ→∞ 1/xⁿ=0, n>0

Polynomial degree rule

limₓ→∞ P(x)/Q(x) depends on degrees

Numerator lower degree

deg P<deg Q ⇒ lim P/Q=0

Equal degrees

deg P=deg Q ⇒ lim P/Q=leading coefficient ratio

Numerator higher degree

deg P>deg Q ⇒ generally ±∞ or no finite limit

Exponential decay

limₓ→∞ e⁻ˣ=0

Exponential growth

limₓ→∞ eˣ=∞

Logarithmic growth

limₓ→∞ ln x=∞

Log vs power

limₓ→∞ (ln x)/xᵃ=0, a>0

Power vs exponential

limₓ→∞ xⁿ/eˣ=0, n>0

Exponential dominates powers

limₓ→∞ eˣ/xⁿ=∞, n>0

7. Methods for Evaluating Limits

Direct substitution

Put x=a if the function is continuous at a

Factorization

Factor numerator and denominator → cancel common factor → substitute

Rationalization

Multiply by conjugate to remove radicals

Standard-limit method

Convert expression to sin x/x, tan x/x, etc.

Substitution method

Let t=g(x) to transform the limit into a standard form

L'Hôpital's rule

lim f/g = lim f′/g′
For appropriate indeterminate forms and when the theorem's conditions are satisfied.

Useful factorization

x²−a²=(x−a)(x+a)

Cubic factorization

x³−a³=(x−a)(x²+ax+a²)

Conjugate identity

(A−B)(A+B)=A²−B²

8. Indeterminate Forms & Advanced Limits

Indeterminate

Common forms

0/0, ∞/∞, 0·∞, ∞−∞, 0⁰, 1∞, ∞⁰

Convert 0·∞

f·g = f/(1/g) or g/(1/f)

Convert ∞−∞

Combine terms into one fraction or rationalize

Power form

For f(x)^g(x), take ln: y=f^g ⇒ ln y=g ln f

Exponential-power limit

lim f^g = exp[lim(g ln f)]
When the transformed limit exists.

Small-angle equivalence

sin x ~ x, tan x ~ x, 1−cos x ~ x²/2
As x→0.

Exponential equivalence

eˣ−1 ~ x
As x→0.

Log equivalence

ln(1+x) ~ x
As x→0.

Binomial approximation

(1+x)ⁿ≈1+nx for small x

Second-order exponential

eˣ=1+x+x²/2!+x³/3!+...

Second-order sine

sin x=x−x³/3!+x⁵/5!−...

Second-order cosine

cos x=1−x²/2!+x⁴/4!−...
