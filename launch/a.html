⚠️ Medical & Website Safety

Medical emergencies

If someone has severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, signs of stroke, major bleeding, loss of consciousness or another life-threatening emergency, contact local emergency services immediately. Do not delay emergency care to search websites.

In India, the pan-India emergency number is 112. Confirm local service availability where you live.

Official India Emergency Response Support System

Check medical information

  • Prefer official health ministries, recognised medical institutions, peer-reviewed research and professional clinical guidelines.
  • Check the date, authors, references, funding and possible conflicts of interest.
  • A research paper or preprint does not automatically mean that a treatment is safe or effective.
  • Do not start, stop or change prescription medicines based only on a website or AI-generated summary.
  • Do not upload identifiable medical records to unknown websites.
  • Be cautious of guaranteed cures, miracle treatments, fake clinical trials and requests for advance payment.

Verify providers and medicines

Confirm doctors' registration with the appropriate medical regulator. Check hospital accreditation where relevant. For drug safety and regulatory notices, consult official regulator websites.
