🔐 Safe Browsing Notes

  • Only open websites you recognize and trust.
  • Check the domain name before entering passwords or personal details.
  • Be cautious of unexpected downloads, payment requests, and sensational headlines.
  • Government websites should be checked carefully for the official domain and HTTPS.
  • Official-looking logos do not guarantee that a website is genuine.
  • News sources may differ in accuracy, editorial perspective, and political viewpoint. Compare multiple credible sources.
  • This launcher is a link directory, not a news publisher or fact-checking service.

ℹ️ About

World News Launcher is a personal website launcher. It opens publications in your browser; it does not host their articles or guarantee their accuracy or availability.

External sites may collect information according to their own privacy policies. Custom links and favorites are stored locally by this application.
