🪔 What makes Indian festivals special?

Festivals bring families and communities together through prayers, music, dance, food, colourful clothing, decorations, charity and traditional rituals.

📅 Festival dates

Many Indian festivals follow lunar or lunisolar calendars. Their Gregorian dates change from year to year. This app gives general seasonal information rather than a verified live calendar.
