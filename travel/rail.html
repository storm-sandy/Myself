Passenger travel checklist

  • Check train running status and schedule before departure.
  • Confirm the train number, boarding station, date and destination.
  • Check ticket status and booking details using the official provider.
  • Verify platform announcements and station displays when you arrive.
  • Use official passenger assistance channels for complaints or help.
  • For luxury and tourist trains, confirm itinerary, inclusions and departure dates with the operator.
Important: This is a railway directory, not an official Indian Railways app. Live running status, seat availability, fares and ticket transactions are handled by the linked providers.
