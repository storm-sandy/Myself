⚙ Settings and preferences

Important travel and privacy notes

  • This is a personal organizer, not a live booking engine.
  • Verify fares, reservations, gates, platforms, timetables, opening hours and permits with official providers.
  • Data is stored in this browser on this device. It does not automatically sync between devices.
  • Private browsing, clearing browser data or device failure may erase saved records.
  • JSON backups may contain personal information. Keep them secure.
  • Never save passwords, payment-card numbers, OTPs or full identity document numbers here.
  • External links open other websites. Check the domain before entering personal or payment information.
  • Notifications and reminders may not work when this page is closed or the operating system suspends the browser.
  • Maps, website directories and live information require an internet connection.
