How to connect

Device A: Open Connect and tap Create Offer. Share its QR code or copy the offer text.

Device B: Scan or paste the offer, then tap Apply Offer & Create Answer. Share the answer back.

Device A: Paste the answer and tap Apply Answer.

Keep both devices online while establishing the connection. QR scanning may require camera permission and a supported browser.

About this demo

This is a browser-based WebRTC prototype. There is no account server, message server, or cloud database.

WebRTC does not guarantee offline delivery. Both devices need to be online to establish a direct connection, and the recipient needs to be connected to receive messages.
