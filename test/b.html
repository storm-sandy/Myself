Connection Steps

  1. Device A creates an offer.
  2. Device A sends the offer to Device B.
  3. Device B pastes the offer and creates an answer.
  4. Device B sends its answer to Device A.
  5. Device A pastes the answer and applies it.
  6. Wait for the connection to open.

🔒 Privacy and Delivery

  • Messages are stored in this browser's IndexedDB.
  • WebRTC encrypts transport between connected peers.
  • Message delivery receipts indicate that a connected peer acknowledged receipt.
  • A receipt does not prove that a person read a message.
  • Offline recipients do not receive messages automatically.
  • Room QR codes are invitations, not authentication credentials.
  • No server-side message backup is provided.
