📘 2. Grammar for Speaking

2.1 Sentence Structure

Basic structure:

Subject + Verb + Object

I + eat + food.
She + reads + books.
They + play + cricket.

For speaking, first become comfortable with simple structures.

🗣️ 5. Pronunciation & Fluency

5.1 Focus on Sounds

Practice difficult sound pairs:

ship / sheep
live / leave
full / fool
fan / van
rice / rise
thin / tin
then / den

Record yourself and compare your pronunciation with a reliable English audio source.

5.2 Word Stress

English is stress-timed. Pay attention to the important syllable in longer words.

TAble
DOCtor
PREsent — noun
preSENT — verb
5.3 Speaking Fluency Technique
  1. Listen to one sentence.
  2. Pause.
  3. Repeat it.
  4. Record yourself.
  5. Compare.
  6. Repeat 3–5 times.

This is commonly called shadowing.

🎯 7. Interview English

7.1 Tell Me About Yourself
Thank you for giving me this opportunity. I have experience in software development and data-related projects. My key strengths are problem solving, SQL, programming and team collaboration. In my current role, I work on understanding requirements, developing solutions, troubleshooting issues and communicating with stakeholders. I am now looking for an opportunity where I can use my experience and continue to grow professionally.
7.2 Strengths
One of my key strengths is problem solving.
I am comfortable working under deadlines.
I communicate clearly with team members.
I take ownership of my responsibilities.
7.3 Weakness
Earlier, I sometimes spent too much time perfecting small details. I have been improving this by prioritizing tasks based on business impact and deadlines.
7.4 Scenario Answer Framework

Use STAR:

S — Situation
What happened?
T — Task
What was your responsibility?
A — Action
What did you do?
R — Result
What happened finally?

🧠 10. Think in English

Do not translate every sentence from your native language.

Look around and describe what you see:

"I am sitting in my room."
"The fan is running."
"I have a meeting at ten."
"I need to finish this task."
"I am feeling tired today."

Start with simple thoughts and gradually make them more complex.

⭐ 11. Fluency Rules

  1. Speak every day.
  2. Do not wait for perfect grammar.
  3. Learn phrases, not only individual words.
  4. Listen to natural English.
  5. Repeat sentences aloud.
  6. Record yourself.
  7. Learn from mistakes.
  8. Use English in real situations.
  9. Think in English for a few minutes every day.
  10. Focus on clarity rather than an artificial accent.
Important: Fluency does not mean speaking very fast. Fluency means expressing your ideas clearly, naturally and confidently.
